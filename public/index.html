Chat

Press Enter (or /) to open the chat, type, and press Enter to send. Esc closes it. On a phone, use the Chat button.

hello!Plain text goes to everyone on the island. /w name messageWhisper to one person. Only they see it. You can use their number instead of the name, like /w 3 hi. /r messageReply to whoever last whispered to you. /whoSee who is on the island, with their numbers. /helpShow the commands in the chat.
