Controls

Click a key, then press the new one. The arrow keys always walk too.

Items

Press 1 to 8 (or tap a slot) to hold that item; press the same number again to put it away. Q moves to the next item (Shift + Q goes back). G drops one at your feet in a sack, Shift + G drops them all. Anyone can pick a sack up with E, so that's how you give things to a friend.

Chat

Press Enter (or /) to open the chat, type, and press Enter to send. Esc closes it. On a phone, use the Chat button.

hello!Plain text goes to everyone on the island. /w name messageWhisper to one person. Only they see it. You can use their number instead of the name, like /w 3 hi. /r messageReply to whoever last whispered to you. /whoSee who is on the island, with their numbers. /helpShow the commands in the chat.

Camera

With a mouse: click the island and the mouse turns the camera. Esc frees it again (and opens this page). The wheel picks the next or previous item; Ctrl + wheel (or a pinch on a trackpad) zooms.
